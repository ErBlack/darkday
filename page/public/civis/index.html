How we work

Signal, resolved.

Most organisations already hold more data than they can use. The gap is not volume, it is resolution: knowing which signals belong to the same person, place or moment.

  • Ingest transactional, mobility, media and survey data through governed pipelines.
  • Resolve entities into a persistent, consent-aware identity graph.
  • Model propensity and segment audiences with explainable features.
  • Activate insights through your existing channels and measure lift.
