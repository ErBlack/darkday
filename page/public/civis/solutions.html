Solutions

Every audience, resolved and understood.

Four capabilities, one governed data foundation. Use them as managed services delivered by our analysts, or run them yourself on the Civis Platform.

Audience Intelligence

Know why, not just what.

Behavioral and psychographic segmentation that turns millions of individual signals into a handful of audiences your teams can actually act on.

  • Psychographic profiling from validated survey instruments fused with observed behavior.
  • Behavioral segmentation refreshed weekly, with stability tracking across waves.
  • Segment explainers in plain language for policy, product and communications teams.
Behavioral Modeling

Predict the next action with confidence.

Propensity, uplift and churn models calibrated per market and monitored in production for drift, bias and coverage.

  • Propensity scoring at individual and household level.
  • Uplift modeling to identify who will respond, not just who will act anyway.
  • Full feature lineage and model cards for every deployed version.
Identity Resolution

One person, one record.

Entity resolution across devices, channels and datasets into a persistent identity graph, with privacy controls built into the matching layer.

  • Deterministic and probabilistic matching with tunable confidence thresholds.
  • Household and location graphs enriched with governed mobility data.
  • Pseudonymisation at ingest, with re-identification restricted to the data owner.
