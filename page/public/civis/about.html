About Civis

We help institutions understand the people they serve.

Civis Vector Systems is an independent analytics company founded in London in 2014 by a group of computational social scientists. Today we are 300 people across four offices, working with public bodies, enterprises and campaigns on some of the hardest questions in applied social data.

Our story

From research lab to global practice.

We started as a university spin-out studying how information moves through populations. Our first clients were public health agencies who needed to know which communities their messages were not reaching.

That question, who is being missed and why, still defines our work. Over a decade we built the tooling to answer it responsibly: resolved identity, behavioral models that can be explained, and governance that regulators can inspect.

We opened in New York in 2017, Belgrade in 2019 and Singapore in 2022. Every office runs the same mix of analysts, engineers and scientists, so a client is never more than one time zone away from the team doing the work.

Principles

How we decide what to build.

Resolution before volume

A smaller dataset that is correctly resolved beats a larger one that is not. We invest in entity resolution first.

Explain every score

If a model cannot show which behaviors drove a prediction, it does not ship. Our clients answer to the public, and so do we.

Consent is infrastructure

Lawful basis, retention and regional processing are encoded in the pipeline, not in a policy document.
