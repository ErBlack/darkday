Body of Young Woman Pulled From the Sava Near Branko's Bridge

Fishermen alerted police at dawn on after spotting a body by the New Belgrade embankment, just below Branko's Bridge. The victim has been identified as A.N. (25), a Belgrade student reported missing at the beginning of October.

Marija Stanković 2 min read 14 comments

Two men fishing from the embankment noticed something caught against the bank under the bridge shortly after six in the morning. A river police unit recovered the body within the hour. Her family had reported her missing on October 1, after she told a friend she was "going to look into something" and never came home.

Investigators have not commented on the cause of death. A source close to the case says there were no signs of a struggle, but several details "do not fit an accident". Police are asking anyone who saw her on the evening of September 30 around Obilićev venac, just across the bridge in the old town, to come forward.

About the case

A.N. (25) went missing on the night of September 30. Her body was found in the Sava by Branko's Bridge days later. The death is being treated as suspicious.
