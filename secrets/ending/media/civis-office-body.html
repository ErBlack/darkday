Man Found Dead Inside Civis Offices in Central Belgrade

Police searching the Civis building on Obilićev venac after an anonymous tip found the body of an employee in a server room late on . The company itself is now under investigation.

Marija Stanković 2 min read 27 comments

Officers arrived shortly before midnight with a warrant, acting on an anonymous tip that the company had been collecting personal data without authorisation. In one of the server rooms they found a man lying between the equipment racks. He has been identified as T.A. (39), a Civis software engineer.

A source close to the case says several systems in the room had been shut down by hand, and a laptop not registered with the company lay next to the body. Prosecutors have seized servers and documents and opened an investigation into the company's activities. Civis said it was "cooperating fully" and declined further comment.

About the case

T.A. (39) was found dead in the company's Belgrade office on the night of , during a search prompted by an anonymous tip. Prosecutors are investigating both his death and the company's activities.
